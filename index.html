Family tree

What you see is scoped to who's logged in: your own line is always visible, in‑laws are visible, but an in‑law's own family of origin stays out of view unless they choose to share it. Each person is labeled with how they relate to you, worked out from the tree itself — real enough to tell a half‑sibling from a full one, or a stepparent from a parent, without anyone typing that in by hand. A dotted branch means "through one parent only."
